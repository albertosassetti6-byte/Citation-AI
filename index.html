    What is Schema.org and how to get cited

    Schema.org is the shared structured-data vocabulary created by Google, Microsoft, Yahoo and Yandex. Embedded in pages as JSON-LD, it helps search engines and AIs understand who you are, what you offer and what your content is about. Generative AIs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) favor sources with clear structured data: Schema.org is the #1 lever to get cited.

    About

    Citation AI is an interactive tool that shows how to optimize a keyword to be cited by generative AIs. It combines a simulated citability score with real data retrieved from public APIs (Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikimedia Pageviews) and an Schema.org JSON-LD snippet generator ready to copy and paste.

    The goal: help you understand what makes content "citable" — structured data, recognizable entities, direct answers and authority — and give you the tools to apply it right away.

    FAQ

    How do I get cited by ChatGPT and Gemini?

    Build a strong entity presence: Schema.org JSON-LD, mentions on authoritative sources, content with direct answers up front, and consistent name/brand across the web.

    Is Schema.org really important for AIs?

    Yes. Structured data is the "native" format crawlers and AI models use to interpret pages. FAQPage and Article are among the most cited schemas in AI answers.

    Where do I place the JSON-LD?

    Inside a <script type="application/ld+json"> tag in the <head> or right before the closing <body> tag. Always validate on validator.schema.org and Google Rich Results Test.

    Are the scores on this site real?

    The "Citation Score" is illustrative and deterministic; instead, Wikipedia, Wikidata and Pageviews shown are real data retrieved via public APIs.

    Is the site responsive?

    Yes, the layout adapts to mobile, tablet and desktop. On small screens the menu becomes a hamburger.
